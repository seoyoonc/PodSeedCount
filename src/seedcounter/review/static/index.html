      Add images

      TIFF files are copied into the input folder and analysed. They then appear in the image list.

      Add annotation

      Choose the raw TIFF and its annotated copy (the full image, with closed white outlines around the seeds), then type the number of seeds in each pod. The program counts the outlined shapes per pod and compares them with your counts.

      Annotations in the ground-truth folder

      Learn from reviews

      Images you marked as checked become ground truth: your accepted, rejected, split and added seeds show the program which seeds are right, and the borders you redrew show it the right seed shapes (accepting a seed does not count as approving its border). The settings are then tuned on them (together with the annotations in ground_truth/).
